# Privacy policy — NTP Clock

NTP Clock is a clock for measuring video latency. It does not have accounts, ads, or analytics.

## Camera

The camera is used only when you tap the inset or press Space, to photograph another screen. That picture is shown beside this clock so the delay can be read. It stays in memory for that comparison. It is not saved to storage, not added to your photo library, and not sent off the device. Leaving the comparison drops it.

## Network

If you allow it, the app asks public NTP servers for the time. The query is a standard time request. It does not include your name, account, location, or the photographs. If you refuse, the app shows the device clock and does not contact a time server.

The servers are time.cloudflare.com, time.google.com, time.windows.com, and pool.ntp.org.

## What is stored on the device

The app remembers whether you allowed the network and the camera, and the last successful time offset, so the clock can keep running. That stays in the app's private settings.

## Children

The app is a clock. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information.
